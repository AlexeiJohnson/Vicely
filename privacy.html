Privacy Policy

Vicely — last updated 17 November 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This policy describes how the Vicely application ("the app") handles data. Vicely is a private desktop application that its owner runs on their own computer to prepare short videos about Grand Theft Auto VI from Rockstar Games' official trailers and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channel, youtube.com/@VicelyGTA6. The app is not offered to the public: it has no user accounts, no sign-up and no website login, and the only person who signs in to it with a Google account is the channel owner. Contact: business.minds.daily1@gmail.com.

2. Google user data the app accesses

When the owner connects their YouTube channel, the app asks Google (OAuth 2.0) for these permissions only:

ScopeWhat it allowsWhy the app needs it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadUpload videos to the connected channel.To publish the owner's finished videos to the owner's channel.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonlyRead the connected channel's basic information.To read the channel's ID, title and handle and show the owner which channel is connected, so videos never go to the wrong channel.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-sslManage the channel's videos and captions.To add captions and a custom thumbnail to the videos the app itself uploaded.

The data the app actually stores is: the OAuth refresh token, short-lived access tokens, and the connected channel's ID, title and handle. It also keeps, for each video it uploaded, the YouTube video ID and the title, description, tags and publish time it set.

The app does not read or store the owner's email address, contacts, private messages, comments, subscribers, watch history, playlists, analytics or payment information, and it never accesses any other person's Google or YouTube data.

3. How the data is used

  • to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel at the times the owner chose;
  • to set each uploaded video's title, description, tags, captions and thumbnail;
  • to show the owner which channel is connected and whether each upload succeeded.

Google user data is used for nothing else: not for advertising, not for building profiles, not sold, and not used to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Content the app creates

The videos are cut from Rockstar Games' official, publicly released trailers and screenshots of Grand Theft Auto VI, with captions, music and text added. The app does not generate images or videos of real people, and it never creates sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery (AI NCII) of anyone. Vicely is a fan channel, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

5. Where the data is stored and how it is protected

All of the data above is stored only on the owner's own computer, in the app's local database, which only the owner can open (behind the owner's own login). The app has no cloud server of its own: the data is sent only to Google, over encrypted HTTPS connections, when the app calls the YouTube Data API. Access tokens are kept in memory and expire within one hour; the refresh token is kept locally so that scheduled uploads can run, and it is never written to logs or shared.

6. Sharing and disclosure

The app does not sell, rent, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. The only party that receives it is Google itself, as part of the YouTube Data API requests described above.

Limited Use. Vicely's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

The data is kept only while the channel is connected. Pressing "Disconnect YouTube" in the app revokes the token with Google and deletes the stored tokens and channel information from the computer immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google account's third-party access page. Records of the videos the app uploaded (their YouTube IDs and texts) can be deleted by the owner at any time. To ask about or request deletion of any data, write to business.minds.daily1@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is used only by its adult owner and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app starts to use Google data in a different way, this page will be updated before that happens and the date at the top will change.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: business.minds.daily1@gmail.com · Home page: alexeijohnson.github.io/Vicely
