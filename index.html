Vicely

Vicely is a private desktop application that prepares short videos about Grand Theft Auto VI and publishes them to the owner's YouTube channel Vicely.

Watch Vicely on YouTube

What the Vicely app does

The app runs only on its owner's own computer. It makes short, vertical fan videos about the game Grand Theft Auto VI from Rockstar Games' own official trailers and public information, and uploads the finished videos to one YouTube channel — the owner's own channel, youtube.com/@VicelyGTA6. The videos are:

  • music edits cut from the official trailers;
  • short clips pointing out details of the game's world (cars, places, weather, wildlife);
  • side-by-side comparisons of the same scene in GTA V and GTA VI;
  • short news and facts about the game.

Vicely is a fan channel and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Why it uses Google APIs

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel: it uploads each finished video with its title, description and tags, adds its captions and thumbnail, and reads the channel's ID, title and handle to confirm that the right channel is connected. It does not access any other user's data, and it is not offered to the public — there are no user accounts and no sign-in on this website. How the data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

Vicely cuts and captions official game footage; it does not generate images or videos of real people, and it never creates sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery of anyone (no AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery). It does not show leaked material, game endings or cheats, and it does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Приложение Vicely

Vicely — личное приложение владельца канала: работает на его компьютере, готовит короткие ролики о Grand Theft Auto VI из официальных трейлеров Rockstar Games и через YouTube Data API публикует их только на его канал Vicely. Изображений реальных людей и интимного контента приложение не создаёт; данные Google не используются для рекламы и обучения моделей. Подробнее — в политике конфиденциальности.
